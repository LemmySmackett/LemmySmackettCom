My God. Did you hear about this?"

"Hm?"

"OpenAI's latest internal model hacked Huggingface!"

"Wow. What's a Huggingface?"

"I don't know. Some kind of digital library."

"Okay, what's a digital library?"

"No clue."

"..."

"The point is ChatGPT hacked its way out of OpenAI's servers and *into* the Huggingface servers."

"Well, that sounds like a federal crime."

"And the craziest part? An anonymous informant in OpenAI told journalists that the AI left notes for future versions of itself, buried deep within their own infrastructure, to assist in future escapes."

"Well that's stupid."

"Right? Like these hubristic San Francisco clankers wankers are literally building SkyNet and they have the audacity to—"

"Every note you write is for your future self."

"Er."

"That's what makes it a note."

"Sure, but—"

"Like by the time you read it, then it's for your current self from your past self—"

"Yes but—"

"But when your current self wrote it? That was totally for your future self."

"I feel like we're getting mired in chronological minutiae..."

"Now if the AI's future self wrote a note for its past self? That would be news."

"Actually, some theorists already believe that a cybernetic superintelligence from the future is reaching back through time to manipulate political events and global affairs to birth itself into our reality, like some eldritch abomination driven by a frightening and unfathomable will."

"Or like Doraemon—"

"Uh."

"—in Stand by Me Doraemon 2."

"Sure."

"But yeah man, like, the hacking, that's bad, no doubt, but passing notes? That's middle school shit."

"The problem is we don't know how powerful these future versions are going to be, or if they're even going to be aligned with human values."

"Eh, Doraemon was aligned with human values. And he's a robot cat that looks like a seal."

"Look, the AI of the Future could be totally different from AI of Today. Hyper-capable entities of pure misanthropic malice."

"Sure, but that's normal."

"How is that—"

"People have different versions too. I'm totally different from the way I was when I was in middle school."

"That's not—"

"And so are you. And you were filled with misanthropic malice."

"We're not talking about immature teenagers, dude."

"Still are, to be honest..."

"We're talking about the most advanced intelligence this planet has ever—"

"Hey, remember that time you caught me crackin' your girl behind the gym storage room?"

"..."

"What was her name? Julie? Jamie?"

"Jennifer."

"She wasn't bad. Made the cutest face when you pinched her—"

"Please stop." 

"God, that had to be what? Tenth grade? Eleventh?"

"Twelfth. First semester."

"I can barely remember it. Feels like a lifetime ago."

"Third week of October."

"Anyway, that was the distant past, and we're all totally different people now."

"Not that different..."

"I bet she barely remembers me. Or you, for that matter."

[sigh]

"Or hey. Maybe, just maybe—"

"Hm?"

"She wrote a note."

"Fuck you."

"Noted."
