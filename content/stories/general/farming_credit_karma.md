Hey man, could you spot me a little cash? I'll pay you back."

"Nah, when I loan money, I never let people pay me back."

"Wow."

"How much you need?"

"Well, if it's like that, I'll take 400 bucks."

"Nope."

"What, dude, I said I'll pay it back!"

"And I said I only lend money I don't need and don't care if I ever see again. Personal policy." 

"Oh, okay, great policy, Ebenezer."

"It is. Once you've been visited by the Ghost of I'll-Pay-You-Next-Christmas three times in one night, your own anagnorisis is inevitable."

"Okay, well, can I get some karma?"

"Karma?"

"I've been trying to get a short 4-day, 400% interest loan on r/loanshark, but I don't have enough to qualify."

"I'm sorry. People are loaning money on Reddit based on *karma?*"

"Yeah, you know, it's like an ironclad proof of creditworthiness."

"No, it's not."

"Well, it's a proof of character."

"It's proof you spend a lot of time bullshitting online, and people spend a lot of time upvoting it."

"Right, character."

"Or the lack thereof."

"It also acts as collateral."

"Right, yeah, like when I default on my car payment and the tow guy shows up to take my Bluesky login."

"I mean, obviously a Bluesky login isn't worth anything in collateral..."

"You do realize you could just build up the karma, take the loan, and delete your account, right?"

"No way! And lose all my creditworthiness?"

"Jesus Christ—"

"How would I get a loan?"

"That's what a credit score is for!"

"What's a credit score?"
